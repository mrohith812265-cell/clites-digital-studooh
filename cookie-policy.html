1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy applies to the Clites website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information more generally.

We have described the current position based on an inspection of the website’s code and the resources it loads. If we introduce new cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before using optional technologies.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can place on your device. They can be used to remember settings, keep a session active, measure how a website is used or support advertising. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels and scripts that store or read information from your browser.

Cookies may be first-party (set by the website you are visiting) or third-party (set by another domain whose resource is loaded by the page). They may last for a single browsing session or for a longer period.

3. What this website uses

At the date of this policy, the website does not set first-party cookies for analytics, advertising, profiling or personalization. Our own scripts do not use localStorage or sessionStorage to track visitors.

The project inquiry form connects to Supabase only when the form is opened or submitted. The Supabase client is configured without persistent authentication sessions, so the form does not create a user account or store login tokens in your browser for this website.

Because the website does not currently use optional cookies or tracking technologies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner. We would rather not show a control that has no real choice behind it. If optional technologies are added later, the policy and consent approach will be updated first.

4. Essential cookies

We do not currently use essential cookies. The website does not have a login area or a shopping basket that needs a cookie to work.

If an essential cookie becomes necessary in future, it will be limited to what is required to provide the requested feature or keep the website secure.

5. Analytics and advertising

We do not currently use Google Analytics, advertising cookies, remarketing tags, social-media pixels or similar cross-site tracking tools on this website. We do not sell advertising space or build advertising profiles from website visits.

If we decide to use privacy-respecting analytics in future, we will review whether consent is required, update this policy and avoid collecting more information than needed.

6. Third-party resources

Some pages load fonts and icons from external providers, including Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs. These requests are used to display the website correctly. The providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page from which the request was made, and their own privacy policies apply.

We do not control cookies or similar technologies that a third-party provider may use independently. The website also contains links to external services such as WhatsApp and email. Visiting those services is subject to their own cookie and privacy practices.

7. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:

  • view the cookies stored on your device;
  • delete some or all cookies;
  • block third-party cookies; or
  • ask the browser to notify you before accepting a cookie.

Because this website does not currently rely on first-party cookies, blocking cookies should not prevent you from reading its pages. Blocking third-party resources may affect the appearance of externally delivered fonts or icons, but the content should remain available.

Clearing cookies in your browser may also clear preferences you have set on other websites. Use your browser’s help pages for instructions specific to your version.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if we change the website or the technologies it uses. The revised version will be published here with a new “Last updated” date. Where required, we will ask for consent before using new optional cookies or similar technologies.
