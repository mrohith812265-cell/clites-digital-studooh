Refund & Cancellation

This policy explains the general approach to cancellations and refunds for web development projects. The written project agreement remains the primary source of project-specific terms.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to website development and related digital services provided by Clites. It explains the general principles we use when a project is cancelled or a refund is requested. It does not create a standard price, payment schedule or refund entitlement for every project.

Important: this page does not set a fixed refund percentage, cancellation fee, deposit rule or notice period. Those matters must be confirmed in the written project agreement and remain subject to applicable law.

2. Project scope and written terms

Website development projects are based on an agreed scope. Before work begins, Clites and the client should confirm the deliverables, timeline, fees, payment schedule, revision limits and cancellation arrangements in writing.

If the scope changes during a project, the effect on fees and timelines should be agreed before the additional work is carried out. A description on this website is not a substitute for that written agreement.

3. Payment schedules

Project costs and payment schedules are confirmed separately in writing. This website does not establish standard instalments, deposit amounts, late-payment charges or taxes. Any applicable taxes and invoicing details should be stated in the project agreement.

Please keep the written proposal, agreement and payment receipts for your records.

4. Cancelling a project

If you need to cancel or pause a project, please contact us as soon as possible using the contact details below. A cancellation request should identify the project and be confirmed in writing so that both sides have a clear record.

After receiving a request, we will review the work completed, the stage of the project, any third-party commitments already made and the terms of the project agreement. We will provide a written summary of the position and discuss the available options.

Any cancellation charge, notice period or non-refundable amount will be the one stated in the written project agreement, subject to applicable law. This policy does not create a separate cancellation fee.

5. Cancellation by Clites

If we are unable to continue with a project for reasons within our control, we will discuss the situation with the client and review the work completed and any amounts paid for work not yet delivered. Any refund or alternative arrangement will be handled in line with the project agreement and applicable law.

We may also need to pause or end work where a client does not provide required materials or approvals, where communication breaks down, or where continuing would require us to act unlawfully or unprofessionally. We will explain the reason in writing where reasonably possible.

6. Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the written project agreement, the work already completed, the payment terms, third-party costs already incurred and applicable law. There is no automatic entitlement to a full refund simply because a project is cancelled.

Where a refund is approved, we will confirm the amount and basis in writing. Refunds will normally be made to the original payment method where practicable, subject to the payment provider’s processing times. Amounts relating to work already completed or services already delivered may be retained where the agreement and applicable law permit.

If you believe a refund is due, please contact us before starting a payment dispute so that we can review the matter with you.

7. Work outside the agreed scope

Additional pages, features, revisions or integrations that fall outside the agreed scope may require a separate estimate or written change to the project agreement. Work outside scope is not included in the original fee unless the parties confirm that in writing.

8. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under applicable consumer-protection or other mandatory law. If a statutory right applies to you, it remains unaffected by this policy or by a project agreement to the extent of any inconsistency.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, project process or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published here with a new “Last updated” date. The policy that applies to a particular project is the version in effect when the project agreement is made, unless the agreement says otherwise.
