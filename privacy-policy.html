1. Who we are

Clites is a web development agency based in Tamil Nadu, India. We provide website design, development and related digital services to startups, small businesses and other clients.

For the purposes of this policy, Clites is responsible for the personal information described below. References to “we”, “us” and “our” mean Clites. This website is a general business website; it is not a registered-company portal, and nothing on it should be read as a representation that Clites holds a particular registration or certification.

2. Information we collect

Information you submit through the project inquiry form

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, business name, project type, budget range and a description of your project requirements. Name, email address and a project message are required; the other fields are optional. When you submit the form, these details are sent to our database provider, Supabase, so that we can review and respond to your inquiry.

Information you provide when you contact us directly

If you email, call or message us using the details published on this website, we may receive your name, contact details and the contents of your message. If you contact us through WhatsApp, your use of that service is also governed by WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

Technical information

Our web server or hosting provider may automatically process standard technical information needed to deliver the website and keep it secure, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, referring page and the time of your request. We do not currently use analytics, advertising pixels or cross-site tracking tools on this website.

Payment information

This website does not collect payment card details. Any invoicing or payment arrangements for a project are handled separately through the channels agreed in writing with the client.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not currently use first-party analytics or advertising cookies. A small number of third-party font and icon resources may be requested by the page; the providers of those resources may receive standard technical request data. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, such as identity documents, financial account details or health information, through the project inquiry form unless we specifically ask for it and explain why it is needed.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • respond to inquiries and discuss potential website development projects;
  • provide services, prepare proposals and manage project communication;
  • send project updates and respond to questions or support requests;
  • maintain business records and manage our relationship with clients and prospective clients;
  • improve the content, functionality and usability of this website;
  • maintain website security and prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorized access; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, we may rely on your consent, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, the performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or other lawful grounds available to us.

We do not use inquiry details to send unrelated marketing messages. If we would like to add you to a marketing list in future, we will ask separately and provide a way to opt out.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate this website and respond to inquiries, including:

  • Supabase, which provides the database and API used by the project inquiry form;
  • website hosting and content-delivery providers;
  • email, telephone and messaging providers used to communicate with you;
  • font and icon delivery providers whose resources are loaded by the website; and
  • professional advisers, where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting or business purposes.

These providers receive information only where it is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, and their own terms and privacy policies may apply. Some providers may process information in countries other than India. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to handle the information consistently with applicable law.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law, a court order or a lawful government request, or if disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of Clites, our clients or others.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. These measures include restricting database access and limiting the information collected through the website.

No method of transmission over the internet and no storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise absolute security. If you have reason to believe that your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us using the details below.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received. Inquiry records may be kept while we respond to a request, evaluate a potential project and maintain ordinary business records. If a project proceeds, relevant records may be kept for the duration of the project and for a reasonable period afterwards for business, accounting and legal purposes.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably practicable, subject to any legal or record-keeping requirements that require us to keep it longer.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold personal information about you and provide access to it;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete information that is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected; or
  • withdraw consent where our processing is based on your consent.

To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may be retained where we have a legitimate or legal reason to do so, and some rights may be limited by applicable law. We will explain the reason if we cannot comply with a request.

You may also raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority in your jurisdiction, where applicable.

8. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided information through this website, please contact us so that we can review and delete it.

9. Third-party services and links

This website may link to third-party websites and services, such as WhatsApp, Google services, social media platforms or other sites that we do not control. Their privacy policies and terms apply to information you provide to them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

We also use external providers to deliver fonts and icons. A request for those resources may disclose standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to the relevant provider.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Where a change is material, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to the attention of affected users.
